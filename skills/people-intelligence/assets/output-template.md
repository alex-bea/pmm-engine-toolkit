## 1. Summary

State the current professional context, the most material active consideration, and the most relevant collaboration dependency. Label each material statement and cite its source and date.

## 2. Current work and decisions

For each active item, record the issue, observed position or action, explicit blocker or dependency, label, source, and date.

## 3. Key professional relationships

List up to five evidence-supported working relationships. For each, state the observed topic and working state: active, waiting on a decision, or blocked by an explicit dependency. Include label, source, and date. Do not infer private relationships or emotional state.

## 4. Observed collaboration preferences

Describe only recurring, professionally relevant preferences supported by at least two independent observations. Explain what appears to help, what may create friction, and any counterevidence. Include labels, sources, and dates.

## 5. Capacity and escalation signals

Include only directly supported professional capacity, urgency, or escalation signals. State the evidence and uncertainty; do not diagnose stress, intent, or personality.

## 6. Practical working guidance

Offer evidence-backed suggestions for framing an update, request, or decision. State what remains uncertain and avoid instructions that depend on private facts.

## 7. From optional long-form notes

Include only when authorized notes were supplied. Cover active asks or commitments, stated priorities, and differences between notes and the primary corpus. Cite each dated note source.

## 8. Changes since the prior brief

Include only in delta mode with a supplied baseline. Identify new, resolved, or less-visible topics, changes in evidence volume or urgency, and changed working relationships. State the baseline date and limitations.

## 9. Relational framing

Include only in relational mode with a supplied reference-person brief or evidence set. Describe evidence-backed intersections, relevant differences, what to emphasize, and what to reframe. Do not infer affinity, conflict, hierarchy, or sensitive relationship information.

## 10. Uncertainties and corrections

List contradictory evidence, low-volume limits, omitted optional inputs, material `[Missing]` facts, and the correction or deletion path.
