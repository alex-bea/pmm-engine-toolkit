# Stakeholder brief workflow

1. Confirm professional purpose, intended audience, and allowed evidence.
2. Exclude sensitive attributes, personal-life speculation, private identifiers, and
   information unrelated to collaboration.
3. Extract stated priorities, decisions, working preferences, commitments, and open loops.
4. Mark each item `Verified`, `Reported`, or `Inference` and cite its source/date.
5. Identify stale or contradictory evidence.
6. Render the brief and include a deletion or correction path.
