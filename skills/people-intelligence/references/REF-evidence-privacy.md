# Evidence and Privacy Guide

## Allowed use

Use only user-supplied, repository-local, public, or explicitly approved professional evidence for a stated collaboration or meeting-preparation purpose. The adopter must decide whether they have a valid consent or other authority basis, who may see the draft, how long it may be retained, and how correction or deletion requests are handled.

## Excluded material

Do not collect or infer sensitive attributes, protected characteristics, health, disability, religion, politics, family or private life, location, compensation, performance rating, employment suitability, or personal personality traits. Do not use this skill for surveillance, disciplinary decisions, ranking people, relationship speculation, or voice imitation.

## Evidence practice

Keep a source and date beside each material claim. Distinguish `Verified`, `Reported`, `Inference`, and `[Missing]`. A recurring professional preference or pattern needs at least two independent observations. Preserve contradictory evidence and state what is uncertain. A short, unusual, unanswered, or high-volume message is not by itself evidence of a motive, pressure state, or trait.

## Systems and storage

External communication, document, directory, and calendar services are optional adopter-owned adapters. Their permissions do not authorize publication, messaging, scheduling, or modification. Keep credentials, source identifiers, evidence corpora, prior briefs, output files, and retention records outside the installed package. The package must contain only blank templates and fictional examples.

## Corrections and deletion

Every completed brief must state a correction or deletion path for the subject or authorized owner. Correct the source record before revising conclusions. When evidence or authority is withdrawn, do not reuse it in a later brief.
