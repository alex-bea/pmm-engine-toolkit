# Manage-up workflow

1. Confirm the reporting window, manager relationship, and desired output channel.
2. Read only approved local evidence and classify it as outcome, active work, risk,
   decision, ask, or context.
3. Deduplicate by outcome and rank by decision value to the manager.
4. Draft a concise update: what changed, why it matters, what is at risk, what decision or
   help is needed, and what the author will do next.
5. Label inferences and missing evidence.
6. Request explicit approval before sending or scheduling anything.
