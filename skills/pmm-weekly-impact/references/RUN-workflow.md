# Weekly impact workflow

1. Define the week and collect approved notes, shipped artifacts, decisions, and metrics.
2. Group evidence by outcome rather than by activity source.
3. For each group, distinguish output, observed outcome, inferred contribution, and unknowns.
4. Identify the most consequential decision, learning, risk, and unresolved dependency.
5. Recommend no more than three next bets with a rationale and validation signal.
6. Render the review with explicit attribution limits.
