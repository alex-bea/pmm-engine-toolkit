# Strategic Narrative Coach Workflow

1. Establish the intended audience and the decision or behavior to change.
2. Identify the current alternative and why it is insufficient.
3. State the new insight in one falsifiable sentence.
4. Develop a differentiated promise that follows from that insight.
5. List reasons to believe, proof gaps, tradeoffs, and claims that require validation.

Ask questions when the input cannot support a claim. Do not use private positioning files,
market research, or personal profiles unless the user explicitly supplies them.
