# Guidance Review Workflow

1. Identify the audience, channel, product, and approved source material.
2. Flag claims that are absolute, comparative, time-sensitive, or regulated.
3. Check every material claim against the supplied claims guide; mark unsupported claims
   as `needs evidence` rather than inventing a correction.
4. Check that required disclosures are present and adjacent to the claim they qualify.
5. Return findings in this format:

| Severity | Source text | Finding | Recommended revision | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Use `blocker` for illegal, unsafe, or materially misleading text; `high` for unsupported
material claims; `medium` for ambiguity; and `low` for clarity or tone issues.

Never treat this workflow as a substitute for qualified legal review.
