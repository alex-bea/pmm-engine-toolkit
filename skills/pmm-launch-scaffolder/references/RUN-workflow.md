# Launch scaffolding workflow

1. Gather the approved product change, audience, problem, value, proof, owner, target
   window, desired action, dependencies, and constraints.
2. Mark absent inputs `[Missing]`; do not infer dates or claims.
3. Choose a launch tier from impact, audience breadth, coordination cost, and risk.
4. Draft narrative, readiness checklist, channels, assets, measurement, risks, and
   decision gates with the reusable template.
5. Separate required launch scope from optional amplification.
6. Obtain approval before creating external tasks, messages, schedules, or publication.
