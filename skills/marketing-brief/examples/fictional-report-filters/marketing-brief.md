# Marketing Brief: Reuse Weekly Reports Instantly

> **Fictional example:** This completed brief is supported only by the accompanying fictional
> source packet and is not evidence for a real organization or product.

## 1. Brief Info

- **Owner:** Maya Chen
- **Launch Name:** Reuse Weekly Reports Instantly
- **Product / Campaign:** Northstar Analytics Saved Views
- **Business Unit:** Product Marketing
- **Tier / Priority:** Tier 2
- **Launch Date:** October 15, 2026

## 2. Launch Summary

Operations teams can save and reopen recurring report views instead of rebuilding filters
each week. Pro and Enterprise workspace members can store filters, columns, and sort order,
then share views with teammates under existing workspace permissions.

## 3. Audience and Problem

- **Primary Audience:** Operations team leads
- **Secondary Audience:** Analytics administrators
- **Core Problem:** Teams repeatedly rebuild the same report filters, columns, and sorting.
- **Why Now:** Beta users cut median weekly report setup from fourteen minutes to six.

## 4. Launch Scope and Value

- **Scope:** Saved Views stores filters, columns, and sort order for reuse and workspace sharing.
- **Customer Value:** Teams start recurring reporting from an approved view instead of rebuilding it.
- **Proof Point 1:** Nine of twelve beta workspaces created multiple views.
- **Proof Point 2:** Median weekly setup fell from fourteen to six minutes.
- **Proof Point 3:** Shared views inherit existing workspace permissions.

## 5. Messaging

- **Topline Message:** Save recurring report views once, then reuse them every week.
- **Support Point 1:** Keep filters, columns, and sorting together.
- **Support Point 2:** Share approved views without changing workspace permissions.
- **Support Point 3:** Open recurring reports without rebuilding the setup.
- **CTA:** Create your first Saved View.

## 7. Success

- **Goal 1:** Reach 120 Saved Views in thirty days.
- **Goal 2:** Forty teams reuse a Saved View weekly.
- **Goal 3:** Increase Pro feature activation by fifteen percent.
