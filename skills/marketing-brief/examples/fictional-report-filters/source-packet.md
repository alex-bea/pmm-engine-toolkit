## Approved product specification

- Product: Northstar Analytics Saved Views.
- Availability: Pro and Enterprise workspaces on October 15, 2026.
- Users can save a report's filters, visible columns, and sort order as one reusable view.
- Users can reopen a saved view when they prepare recurring reports.
- Workspace members can share a saved view with teammates.
- Shared views inherit existing workspace permissions; the launch adds no new permission
  level.
- The launch does not include scheduled delivery or automated report generation.

## Approved launch plan

- Owner: Maya Chen.
- Business unit: Product Marketing.
- Launch date: October 15, 2026.
- Tier: Tier 2.
- Primary audience: operations team leads.
- Secondary audience: analytics administrators.
- Core problem: teams repeatedly rebuild the same report filters, columns, and sorting.
- Channels: product blog, lifecycle email, in-product announcement, LinkedIn, and customer
  success enablement.
- Thirty-day goal: 120 Saved Views created.
- Weekly-use goal: 40 teams reopen at least one Saved View each week.
- Product goal: increase Pro feature activation by 15 percent within 30 days.

## Approved messaging note

- Approved feature name: Saved Views.
- Do not call the feature "reusable filters."
- Topline: Save recurring report views once, then reuse them every week.
- Support: keep filters, columns, and sorting together.
- Support: share approved views without changing workspace permissions.
- Support: open recurring reports without rebuilding the setup.
- CTA: Create your first Saved View.

## Approved beta review

- Twelve fictional beta workspaces participated.
- Nine beta workspaces created at least two Saved Views.
- Median weekly report setup time fell from 14 minutes to 6 minutes among participating
  workspaces.
- The beta review did not measure revenue, retention, or customer satisfaction.
