# Marketing Brief Workflow

## Source precedence

Prefer approved product specifications and launch decisions, then approved positioning,
then customer research and internal notes. If sources conflict, record the conflict and
ask for a decision.

## Brief template

```markdown
# [Launch] Marketing Brief

## Audience
## Problem
## What changed
## Customer value
## Proof and constraints
## Positioning
## Launch tier and channels
## Risks and approvals
## Open questions
```

Use a launch tier only to calibrate effort: Tier 1 is a major audience or product change,
Tier 2 is a meaningful feature or segment expansion, and Tier 3 is a minor update. Do not
invent dates, metrics, claims, customer names, or proof points.
