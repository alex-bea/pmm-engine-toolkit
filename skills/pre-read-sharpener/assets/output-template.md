# Pre-Read Sharpener Output Template

## Global writing rules

- The tightened rewrite is at most 600 words. The review, cuts, agenda, and self-check are
  outside that count.
- Use plain English, active voice, and a direct, professional tone.
- Use only facts from the supplied draft. Never invent evidence, dates, metrics, people,
  options, claims, or consensus.
- Write `[Missing]` for absent decision-critical information.
- Bold at most one key phrase per paragraph.
- Do not use em dashes in the final rewrite.
- Keep paragraphs to four rendered lines or fewer.
- Keep names, links, and dates exactly as supplied.

## Tightened rewrite template

```markdown
# <Verb-first, decision-framed title, 10 words maximum>

**Audience:** <Named role or roles; never a generic group>
**Decision needed:** <One sentence, 25 words maximum>
**Recommended:** <One option, 15 words maximum>

## TL;DR

- <Recommendation in plain language, 25 words maximum>
- <Strongest supported reason, 25 words maximum>
- <Cost or risk being accepted, 25 words maximum>

## The decision

<100 words maximum. The first sentence contains the decision-forcing question. State why
the decision is needed now and recommend one option.>

## Tradeoffs

| Option | Pros | Cons | Cost to reverse |
|---|---|---|---|
| ✓ <Recommended option, 8 words maximum> | <20 words maximum> | <20 words maximum> | <Low, Medium, or High plus a 10-word maximum note> |
| <Rejected option, 8 words maximum> | <20 words maximum> | <20 words maximum> | <Low, Medium, or High plus a 10-word maximum note> |
| <Optional second rejected option, 8 words maximum> | <20 words maximum> | <20 words maximum> | <Low, Medium, or High plus a 10-word maximum note> |

## What changes if we say yes

<80 words maximum. State concrete supported changes across the source's named time
horizons. Avoid abstract claims.>

## Open questions (if any)

- <Only a question that blocks the decision, 20 words maximum>
- <Optional second blocking question, 20 words maximum>
```

## Template rules

- Use exactly three TL;DR bullets.
- Put the recommended option first in the tradeoff table and prefix it with `✓`.
- Use two or three option rows total.
- Keep the Recommended field and checked table row consistent.
- Always include Cost to reverse. Use `[Missing]` when the source does not provide it.
- Omit Open questions entirely when no question blocks the decision.
- The first 100 words plus the tradeoff table must reveal the decision and accepted cost.
