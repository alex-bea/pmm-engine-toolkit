## Blunt PM review

The draft is asking three executives to approve a four-week beta expansion from eight to 24
workspaces. Its evidence and rollback path are strong, but the decision is buried under
background and the options are not directly comparable. The biggest problem is that the
reader must assemble the recommendation, success threshold, accepted support risk, and
reversal cost from separate sections. Put the decision first, make the two choices parallel,
and surface the unresolved coverage question after four support hours without inventing an
owner.

## Biggest issues

- The decision appears after background instead of leading the document.
- The two options use different structures, slowing comparison.
- Success thresholds are separated from the recommendation and follow-up plan.
- The accepted support risk is not visible in the opening.
- The unresolved coverage owner needs explicit treatment.

## Specific cuts

- “Guided Import helps administrators map spreadsheet columns...” Cut product explanation to
  one phrase.
- “The beta currently includes eight workspaces.” Move into decision evidence.
- “Customer Success uses a written setup script...” Move into approved outcome.
- “Decide whether to expand...” Promote to the opening question.
- “Recommendation: expand...” Merge with the opening recommendation.
- “Invite 16 additional workspaces...” Move into the outcome section.
- “Review completion and mapping-related tickets...” Combine with weekly reporting.
- “Tests more spreadsheet layouts...” Keep as the leading benefit.
- “Keeps support demand at the current level.” Keep only in the rejected option.
- “Product and Customer Success will review...” Move into the concrete outcome.

## Tightened rewrite

# Approve the Four-Week Guided Import Expansion

**Audience:** VP Product, VP Customer Success, Head of Engineering  
**Decision needed:** Should we expand Guided Import from eight to 24 beta workspaces on October 5?  
**Recommended:** Expand to 24 workspaces for four weeks.

## TL;DR

- Expand Guided Import to 24 beta workspaces for four weeks.
- Seven of eight current workspaces completed imports, while median setup fell from 42 to 18 minutes.
- We accept more support tickets while holding engineering coverage to four hours per week.

## The decision

Should we expand Guided Import from eight to 24 beta workspaces on October 5? **Approve the
four-week expansion.** The current beta produced seven completed imports across eight
workspaces while median setup fell from 42 to 18 minutes. Sixteen additional workspaces will
test more spreadsheet layouts before the October 30 general-availability review. Do not keep
the beta at eight workspaces because that delays broader layout evidence by four weeks.

## Tradeoffs

| Option | Pros | Cons | Cost to reverse |
|---|---|---|---|
| ✓ Expand to 24 workspaces | Tests more layouts before general-availability review. | May create more mapping-related tickets. | Low, pause invitations within one business day. |
| Keep eight workspaces | Holds support demand at its current level. | Delays broader layout evidence by four weeks. | Low, send prepared invitations within one business day. |

## What changes if we say yes

Customer Success sends 16 invitations and the setup script on October 5. The integration
engineer keeps four support hours per week. Product reports completion and mapping-related
tickets every Friday. On October 30, Product and Customer Success recommend whether to move
toward general availability. Success requires 20 completed imports across 24 workspaces and
no more than six mapping-related tickets from the expanded group.

## Suggested agenda

- Review current beta evidence.
- Accept or reject support risk.
- Assign coverage after four support hours.
