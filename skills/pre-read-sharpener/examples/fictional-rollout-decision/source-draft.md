# Fictional Source Draft: Guided Import Beta Expansion

> **Fictional source:** Harborline Software, its people, product, dates, workspaces, and
> results are invented. This document is not evidence for a real company or product.

Author: Priya Shah, Product Lead  
Meeting: October 1, 2026, 30 minutes  
Audience: VP Product, VP Customer Success, Head of Engineering

## Background

Guided Import helps administrators map spreadsheet columns during first-time data import.
The beta currently includes eight workspaces. Seven completed an import. Median setup time
fell from 42 minutes to 18 minutes. Two workspaces opened one mapping-related support ticket
each.

One integration engineer currently covers the beta for up to four hours per week. Customer
Success uses a written setup script for every invite.

## Proposed decision

Decide whether to expand Guided Import from eight to 24 beta workspaces on October 5, 2026,
for a four-week evaluation ending October 30.

Recommendation: expand to 24 workspaces for four weeks. Invite 16 additional workspaces on
October 5. Review completion and mapping-related tickets every Friday.

## Options

### Expand to 24 workspaces

- Tests more spreadsheet layouts before general availability.
- Keeps engineering coverage at four hours per week.
- Accepts higher ticket volume during the four-week evaluation.
- Can be reversed by pausing new invitations within one business day.

### Keep the beta at eight workspaces

- Keeps support demand at the current level.
- Delays evidence from additional spreadsheet layouts by four weeks.
- Can be reversed by sending the prepared invitations within one business day.

## Success and follow-up

By October 30, success means at least 20 of 24 beta workspaces complete an import and the
expanded group opens no more than six mapping-related tickets. Product and Customer Success
will review results on October 30 before recommending general availability.

If approved, Customer Success sends the 16 invitations and setup script on October 5. The
integration engineer maintains four hours of weekly coverage. Product publishes a weekly
internal completion-and-ticket summary.

Open question: who covers urgent mapping issues after the engineer's four weekly hours are
used?
