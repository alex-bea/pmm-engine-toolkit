# Competitive-intelligence tracker templates

Keep these trackers beside the adopter-owned registry. Append reviewed events; do not rewrite
history to match the latest interpretation.

## Battlecard gaps

Use this tracker when a verified, buyer-relevant competitor claim lacks a strong reviewed
response.

| Gap ID | Opened | Competitor | Verified claim | Affected buyer/use case | Coverage | Needed response | Evidence | Owner | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `[gap-id]` | `[YYYY-MM-DD]` | `[name]` | `[claim]` | `[buyer/use case]` | `WEAK / MISSING / CONFLICTING` | `[counter, proof, pricing, enablement, etc.]` | `[IDs/links]` | `[owner]` | `open` |

Before adding a gap, search open rows for the same competitor and substantially similar claim.
Update the existing row when the needed response is the same.
