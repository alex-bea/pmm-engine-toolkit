# Complete fictional example

Everything in this example is invented. Organizations, products, quotes, events, sources,
people, and results are fictional. Reserved `.invalid` URLs deliberately do not resolve.

The example is a full-depth, non-reversible structural mirror of a mature embedded-wallets
workflow. It preserves the useful document shapes, analytical relationships, stages, and
level of detail while replacing every organization, person, customer, channel, URL, quote,
claim, price, date, and deal fact with invented material. There is no published one-to-one
alias map back to any source workflow.

It shows the full first-run onboarding and limited baseline for fictional adopter HarborKey
and six fictional competitors from 2026-05-01 through 2026-09-01. Read the files in this
order:

| File | What it demonstrates |

## What happens in the run

1. The PMM supplies the HarborKey website, an embedded-wallet infrastructure product scope,
   and six competitor homepages. The agent creates market `harborkey-embedded-wallets`.
2. The agent follows those homepages to propose product, pricing, blog, changelog, release,
   documentation, repository, and social sources. The PMM reviews a URL or a `not found`
   result for all nine source families before the agent writes `source-map.md`.
3. The agent inspects only Slack and Drive metadata, suggests likely channels and documents,
   receives permission for three fictional Slack channels and three fictional Drive files,
   and records one local note source and one community source supplied by the PMM.
4. The agent drafts `adopter-positioning.md` from verified HarborKey sources. The PMM approves
   its audience, problem, category, value, differentiators, claims, proof, and comparison
   criteria before any competitor analysis begins.
5. The agent runs a baseline for 2026-05-01 through 2026-09-01. Collection captures acquisition,
   rebrand, pricing, narrative-change, migration, enterprise-versus-developer, and low-level
   infrastructure patterns across active, monitored, and watchlist competitors.
6. The evidence review accepts attributable records, separates public and internal material,
   records a conflict, and rejects a snippet, a duplicate, a superseded item, and an
   out-of-window item before synthesis begins.
7. Synthesis compares HarborKey with all six competitors and populates detailed active
   profiles, abbreviated monitored profiles, narrative changes, battlecard gaps, evaluation
   signals, watch items, and proposed registry changes.
8. The stakeholder lens elevates signals relevant to migration readiness, policy controls,
   and category expansion. The lens does not alter evidence status, confidence, or safety.
9. The briefing is labeled `LIMITED COVERAGE`, marks premature comparisons, and asks the PMM
   to verify AsterPort's proposed migration guide next.
10. The proposed registry and tracker changes remain pending until the exact draft is reviewed.

## Optional controller example

The JSON fixtures in `fixtures/` exercise the bundled deterministic controller. They are a
separate synthetic mechanical fixture, not the HarborKey market, and remain unchanged. Use
them when testing structured manifests and approvals; they are not required for a normal run.
