# Competitive intelligence workflow

1. Resolve the question, market boundary, competitor set, time window, and approved sources.
2. Capture source URL or path, publication date, observation date, exact claim summary,
   evidence label, and confidence.
3. Separate competitor statements from independently verified facts.
4. Cluster signals into product, positioning, pricing, go-to-market, customer, and talent.
5. Identify implications, counter-evidence, knowledge gaps, and next validation steps.
6. Render a briefing; do not modify battlecards or external systems without approval.
