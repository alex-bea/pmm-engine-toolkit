# Evidence and claims

## Collection method

Collect current product, positioning, pricing, go-to-market, customer, talent, and other
configured signals. Preserve source-native identity, source version, canonical reference,
publication/event/observation dates, query identity, sensitivity, and limitations.

Prefer first-party product pages, documentation, release notes, repositories, pricing, and
official statements. Treat secondary reporting and community discussion as lower authority.
A search snippet is discovery evidence and cannot be verified or solely support a
high-confidence claim.

Keep corroborating sources separate and linked. Keep incompatible credible sources and mark
the conflict. Exact duplicates collapse deterministically; near duplicates retain their
algorithm, score, threshold, and decision. A changed source object becomes a linked revision.

## Claim method

Every material observation, attributed report, inference, and recommendation cites evidence
IDs from the approved manifest. Missing evidence stays missing. Strategic meaning and
stakeholder relevance are analysis, not sourced facts.

Prove narrative change with prior and current text or a structured diff, then label the
strategic implication as inference. Preserve conflicts and caveats. A claim may keep or
increase the sensitivity of its support but may not downgrade it.

Use the market pack's positioning context to identify strong, weak, missing, or conflicting
support. Propose battlecard gaps and other tracker events without mutating them during
synthesis.
