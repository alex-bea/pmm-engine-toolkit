# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PMM Engine Toolkit is committed to a professional, respectful, and harassment-free
community. Participation is open to people of every background, identity, experience
level, and perspective who engage in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, direct, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and artifacts rather than people.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes without retaliation.
- Respect privacy, consent, boundaries, and confidential reporting.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or targeted hostility.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation.

## Scope

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and events, and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post conduct reports publicly. Use GitHub's **Report content** action to report to
repository administrators when available. Otherwise, use
[GitHub's abuse-reporting flow](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or a private contact method listed on the repository owner's GitHub profile.

Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to assess and resolve the report. Based
on context, severity, and pattern, responses may include a private correction, warning,
content removal, temporary participation restriction, or permanent ban. Retaliation or a
knowing bad-faith report may itself result in enforcement.
