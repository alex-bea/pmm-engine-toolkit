# PMM work tracker example

A PMM team can use the generic tracker without adding a separate project-management
service:

```text
roadmap: Go-to-market readiness
└── epic: Launch narrative
    ├── task: Approve the positioning brief
    └── task: Validate the sales enablement draft
```

Each task is small enough to verify independently. A task cannot move to `done` merely
because a draft exists; its `evidence` must demonstrate that the acceptance criteria were
met. Product tags, campaign lanes, and launch phases may be added as optional labels.
